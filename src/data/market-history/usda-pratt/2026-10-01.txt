*** Final Report ***
Compared to last week, feeder steers 800 lb to 975 lb sold 3.00 to 10.00 higher, in places up to 17.00 higher. A lower trend was noted on
steers under 800 lb. Not enough feeder heifers 675 lb to 875 lb for a market test, however a steady trend was noted. A lower trend was
noted on heifers under 675 lb. Demand was good for cattle over 800 lbs. Moderate demand for cattle under 800 lb Slaughter cows and bulls
sold steady with a lower undertone. Supply included: 82% Feeder Cattle (68% Steers, 31% Heifers, 1% Bulls); 15% Slaughter Cattle (96%
Cows, 4% Bulls); 3% Replacement Cattle (92% Bred Cows, 3% Bred Heifers, 5% Cow-Calf Pairs). Feeder cattle supply over 600 lbs was 77%.
