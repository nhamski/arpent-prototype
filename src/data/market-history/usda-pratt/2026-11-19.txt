*** Final Report ***
Compared to last week, feeder steers 600 lb to 950 lb sold 3.00 to 8.00 lower, however a partial load of fancy steers weighing 868 sold 3.00
higher. Feeder heifers 600 lb to 1,000 lb sold unevenly steady. Not enough heifer calves under 600 lb for a market test. Not enough steer and
heifer calves under 600 lb for a market test. Demand was moderate. Slaughter cows sold steady to 3.00 higher. Slaughter bulls sold 5.00 to
6.00 lower. Supply included: 43% Feeder Cattle (57% Steers, 37% Heifers, 6% Bulls); 24% Slaughter Cattle (95% Cows, 5% Bulls); 34%
Replacement Cattle (79% Bred Cows, 20% Bred Heifers, 1% Cow-Calf Pairs, 0% Heifer Pairs). Feeder cattle supply over 600 lbs was 68%.
