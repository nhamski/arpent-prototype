*** Final Report ***
Compared to last week, feeder steers 600 lb to 900 lb sold 4.00 to 12.00 lower. Steer calves 300 lb to 600 lb sold 4.00 to 35.00 higher even
higher on reputation vaccinated steers. Heifers 400 lb to 975 lb sold 4.00 to 12.00 lower. Heifer calves 300 lb to 400 lb sold up to 35.00
higher. Demand was moderate on feeders, good on calves. Slaughter cows and bulls sold 2.00 to 4.00 lower. Supply included: 88% Feeder
Cattle (71% Steers, 29% Heifers, 1% Bulls); 11% Slaughter Cattle (93% Cows, 7% Bulls); 1% Replacement Cattle (63% Bred Cows, 37% Cow-Calf
Pairs). Feeder cattle supply over 600 lbs was 34%.
