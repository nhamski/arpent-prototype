*** Final Report ***
Compared to last week. feeder steers 800 lb to 925 lb sold 3.00 to 4.00 higher. Steers 550 lb to 800 lb sold 20.00 to 25.00 higher. No recent
comparison on steers under 550 lb, however a definte higher trend was noted especially on fancy weaned and vaccinated steer calves.
Feeder heifers 475 lb to 950 lb sold 7.00 to 14.00 higher, in places up to 20.00 higher. No recent comparison on heifer calves under 475 lb,
however a higher trend was noted especially on weaned and vaccinated heifers. Demand was good. Over 2200 calves were traded today.
Slaughter cows and bulls sold steady, however 1 high yielding bull sold for 2.00 Supply included: 95% Feeder Cattle (54% Steers, 45% Heifers,
1% Bulls); 5% Slaughter Cattle (88% Cows, 12% Bulls); 0% Replacement Cattle (20% Bred Cows, 80% Bred Heifers). Feeder cattle supply over
600 lbs was 54%.
