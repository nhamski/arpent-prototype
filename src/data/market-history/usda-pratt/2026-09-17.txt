*** Final Report***
Compared to last week, feeder steers 750 lb to 975 lb sold from 3.00 higher to 3.00 lower. Steers 400 lb to 750 lb sold lower on light receipts.
Feeder heifers 800 lb to 925 lb sold steady, however 2 partial loads of fancy heifers sold 10.00 to 12.00 higher. Heifers 400 lb to 800 lb sold
6.00 to 8.00 higher, however a partial load of thin 699 lb heifers sold for 356 which would be 25.00 higher. Demand was good. Slaughter
cows and bulls sold 2.00 to 4.00 lower. Supply included: 85% Feeder Cattle (55% Steers, 45% Heifers, 0% Bulls); 12% Slaughter Cattle (92%
Cows, 8% Bulls); 3% Replacement Cattle (95% Bred Cows, 3% Bred Heifers, 2% Cow-Calf Pairs). Feeder cattle supply over 600 lbs was 87%.
