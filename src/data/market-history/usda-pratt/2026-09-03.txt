*** Final Report ***
Compared to last week, feeder steers 600 lb to 900 lb sold 4.00 to 12.00 lower, however steers weighing 725 lb to 750 lb sold close to steady.
Steers under 600 lb sold 6.00 to 10.00 higher Not enough heifers 425 lb to 925 lb for a market test, however a lower trend was noted.
Demand was moderate, however demand was good on steers weighing under 600 lb. Slaughter cows and bulls sold steady to 3.00 lower.
Supply included: 73% Feeder Cattle (58% Steers, 40% Heifers, 2% Bulls); 23% Slaughter Cattle (95% Cows, 5% Bulls); 4% Replacement Cattle
(100% Bred Cows). Feeder cattle supply over 600 lbs was 54%.
