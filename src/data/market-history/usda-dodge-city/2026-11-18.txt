Compared to last week, steers 650 lbs to 850 lbs and heifers over 700 lbs sold steady, strong undertone for only a few true grass yearlings
while fleshy cattle sold 5.00 to 10.00 lower. Steers under 650 lbs and heifers under 700 lbs sold steady, strong undertone for large groups of
reputation cattle with multiple vaccinations. Smaller groups sold weak. Boning utility slaughter cows sold steady to 5.00 higher. Lean utility
slaughter cows sold sharply higher in some instances. Supply included: 81% Feeder Cattle (57% Steers, 42% Heifers, 1% Bulls, 0% Beef/Dairy
Steers); 12% Slaughter Cattle (93% Cows, 6% Bulls, 0% Dairy Bulls); 8% Replacement Cattle (20% Stock Cows, 76% Bred Cows, 4% Bred
Heifers). Feeder cattle supply over 600 lbs was 70%.
