* Hall of Fame Sale, Saturday, July 25, 2026*
Compared to last week, feeder and stocker steers and heifers, not enough for market test. Boning utility slaughter cows sold steady, strong
undertone. Lean utility slaughter cows sold steady to 8.00 higher. Several slaughter bulls sold today. Today also marks the 13th day of down
of CME Cattle Futures. 3 loads of heavy 8 weight heifers sold 317.25 to 319.50 cwt, one load of light 9 weight steers sold for 329.00 cwt.
Supply included: 58% Feeder Cattle (31% Steers, 68% Heifers, 1% Bulls); 28% Slaughter Cattle (76% Cows, 24% Bulls); 14% Replacement Cattle
(17% Stock Cows, 74% Bred Cows, 9% Cow-Calf Pairs). Feeder cattle supply over 600 lbs was 84%.
