not enough for market test. Boning utility slaughter cows sold 2.00 to 5.00 lower and Lean utility slaughter cows sold steady to 7.00 lower.

(37% Steers, 61% Heifers, 1% Bulls); 15% Slaughter Cattle (89% Cows, 11% Bulls); 11% Replacement Cattle (12% Stock Cows, 88% Bred Cows).
