Compared to last week, steers and heifers sold steady to 8.00 lower. Demand was moderate. Ranchers are taking a step back and adjusting
to what the market prices have done over the past month. Calf runs will be in the near future now as range conditions have been getting
worse in the extreme heat the past couple weeks. Slaughter cows and bulls sold steady to 3.00 lower. In the hay market, demand and
movement remained steady. Drought conditions across much of the state continue to intensify, placing additional stress on crop and hay
production. Hay supplies remain tight, with significant volumes moving out of state to meet demand. Prices have begun to increase as
available supplies become more limited. Supply included: 82% Feeder Cattle (48% Steers, 51% Heifers, 1% Bulls); 15% Slaughter Cattle (93%
Cows, 7% Bulls, 0% Dairy Bulls); 3% Replacement Cattle (22% Stock Cows, 75% Bred Cows, 3% Cow-Calf Pairs). Feeder cattle supply over 600
lbs was 74%.
