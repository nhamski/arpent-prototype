Compared to last week, steers under 650 lbs sold 5.00 to 10.00 higher; 650-850 lbs 4.00 to 10.00 lower; over 850 lbs uneven, 2.00 lower to
3.00 higher. Heifers under 650 lbs 5.00 to 15.00 higher; over 650 sold 5.00 to 9.00 higher. Demand was moderate to good. Lean Slaughter
cows sold steady to 5.00 higher, while the balance of the cows and slaughter bulls sold steady to 2.00 lower. In the hay market, movement
slowed this past week as much of the state received heavy rainfall. Demand remained high. Recent rainfall and warm temperatures have
many producers anticipating another cutting of alfalfa. According to the US Drought Monitor for the week of October 8th, in the High Plains,
widespread heavy precipitation abetted robust improvement across eastern Colorado, central and western Kansas, and parts of southern
Nebraska. Supply included: 92% Feeder Cattle (59% Steers, 41% Heifers, 1% Bulls); 6% Slaughter Cattle (92% Cows, 8% Bulls); 3%
Replacement Cattle (22% Stock Cows, 70% Bred Cows, 8% Open Heifers). Feeder cattle supply over 600 lbs was 68%.
