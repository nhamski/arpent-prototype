Compared to last week, steers and heifers sold 3.00 to 10.00 higher, with instances up to 15.00 higher in some of the lighter weight
categories. Demand was moderate to good. Slaughter cows and bulls sold steady to 3.00 lower. In the hay market, movement slowed this
past week as much of the state received heavy rainfall. Demand remained high. Recent rainfall and warm temperatures have many producers
anticipating another cutting of alfalfa. According to the US Drought Monitor for the week of October 8th, in the High Plains, widespread heavy
precipitation abetted robust improvement across eastern Colorado, central and western Kansas, and parts of southern Nebraska. Supply
included: 77% Feeder Cattle (59% Steers, 40% Heifers, 1% Bulls); 6% Slaughter Cattle (92% Cows, 8% Bulls); 17% Replacement Cattle (2%
Stock Cows, 55% Bred Cows, 33% Bred Heifers, 10% Cow-Calf Pairs, 1% Heifer Pairs). Feeder cattle supply over 600 lbs was 63%.
