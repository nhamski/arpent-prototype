Compared to last week, steers and heifers sold 2.00 to 5.00 higher, with instances up top 15.00 higher on calves coming off grass and and
green condition. Demand was moderate to good. Slaughter cows and bulls sold 4.00 to 7.00 lower. In the hay market, Demand remained
strong this past week as hay inventories continued to decline. Hay shipments to states experiencing more severe drought conditions
remained active, further tightening supplies in some areas. Much of the state received beneficial moisture over the past week, with additional
precipitation in the forecast. Supply included: 83% Feeder Cattle (60% Steers, 39% Heifers, 1% Bulls); 8% Slaughter Cattle (90% Cows, 10%
Bulls); 9% Replacement Cattle (6% Stock Cows, 77% Bred Cows, 16% Bred Heifers, 1% Cow-Calf Pairs). Feeder cattle supply over 600 lbs was
69%.
