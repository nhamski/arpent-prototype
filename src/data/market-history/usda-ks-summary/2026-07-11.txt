There were not enough cattle on offer of any specific weight group the last two weeks to compare this week with so no trend is applicable.
Compared to last week, trading of Kansas negotiated fed cattle sold 7.00 lower at 248.00. In the hay market, prices and movement remained
steady. With warmer temperatures, abundant sunshine, and windy conditions, fields have dried sufficiently, allowing hay harvest activities to
progress across much of the state. Many producers are in the midst of second cutting alfalfa, while brome harvest is underway in several
areas. Hay season is now in full swing as favorable weather has supported fieldwork. Supply included: 85% Feeder Cattle (68% Steers, 31%
Heifers, 0% Bulls, 0% Beef/Dairy Steers); 12% Slaughter Cattle (88% Cows, 11% Bulls, 1% Dairy Bulls); 3% Replacement Cattle (25% Stock
Cows, 56% Bred Cows, 1% Bred Heifers, 18% Cow-Calf Pairs). Feeder cattle supply over 600 lbs was 87%.
