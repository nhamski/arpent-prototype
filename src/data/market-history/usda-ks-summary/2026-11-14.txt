Compared to last week, steers and heifers sold 5.00 to 15.00 higher, getting back near all of the backslide in prices from the previous week.
Demand was moderate to good. Slaughter cows and bulls sold 3.00 to 5.00 lower. In the hay market, Hay supplies remain tight, with prices
continuing to increase steadily in some areas. Producers are facing increased harvest pressure as they work to complete field activities ahead
of the first freeze. Some feedlots are adjusting rations by incorporating failed corn and milo to reduce alfalfa usage amid elevated prices.
Demand for alfalfa pellets remains strong, while supplies continue to be limited. Supply included: 84% Feeder Cattle (57% Steers, 40%
Heifers, 2% Bulls); 6% Slaughter Cattle (90% Cows, 10% Bulls); 9% Replacement Cattle (3% Stock Cows, 58% Bred Cows, 37% Bred Heifers, 2%
Cow-Calf Pairs). Feeder cattle supply over 600 lbs was 57%.
