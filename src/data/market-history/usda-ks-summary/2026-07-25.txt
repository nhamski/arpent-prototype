Another light volume week of feeder cattle on offer in Kansas. Compared to last week, steers and heifers under 600 lbs not enough on offer
to test. 600 to 800 lb steers sold 8.00 to 15.00 lower, while those over 800 lbs sold 2.00 to 4.00 lower. Feeder heifers 600 to 750 lbs sold
13.00 to 15.00 lower, while those over 750 lbs were unevenly steady. Pressure from the CME Cattle complex and lower boxed beef prices
have caused feeder cattle prices to come off recent record highs. Light trading of slaughter cows and bulls sold steady to 5.00 lower.
Compared to last week, trading of Kansas negotiated fed cattle sold 7.00 to 8.00 lower at 230.00 on light trading. In the hay market, hay
movement remained steady across the state. Many producers have begun third cutting alfalfa, with yields reported as near to below average.
Quality has been reported as very good where producers were able to bale ahead of recent rainfall. Brome hay yields and quality continue to
vary significantly by region, while prairie hay harvest is just beginning in many areas of the state. Supply included: 80% Feeder Cattle (67%
Steers, 31% Heifers, 2% Bulls); 11% Slaughter Cattle (80% Cows, 20% Bulls); 9% Replacement Cattle (8% Stock Cows, 81% Bred Cows, 1% Bred
Heifers, 10% Cow-Calf Pairs). Feeder cattle supply over 600 lbs was 93%.
