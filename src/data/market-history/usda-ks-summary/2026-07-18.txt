Another light volume week of feeder cattle on offer in Kansas. Compared to last week, most weight ranges of steers and heifers sold 0.00 to
15.00 lower. Pressure from the CME Cattle complex and the typical summer slump in beef prices have put a damper on the feeder cattle run-
up at the present time. Compared to last week, trading of Kansas negotiated fed cattle sold 10.00 to 11.00 lower at 237.00 to 238.00. In the
hay market, prices and movement remained steady. With warmer temperatures, abundant sunshine, and windy conditions, fields have dried
sufficiently, allowing hay harvest activities to progress across much of the state. Many producers are in the midst of second cutting alfalfa,
while brome harvest is underway in several areas. Hay season is now in full swing as favorable weather has supported fieldwork. Supply
included: 86% Feeder Cattle (43% Steers, 57% Heifers, 0% Bulls); 10% Slaughter Cattle (76% Cows, 24% Bulls); 5% Replacement Cattle (17%
Stock Cows, 74% Bred Cows, 9% Cow-Calf Pairs). Feeder cattle supply over 600 lbs was 75%.
